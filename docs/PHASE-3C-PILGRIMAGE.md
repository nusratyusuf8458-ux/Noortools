# Phase 3C — Hajj & Umrah Foundation

## Scope

This slice provides a premium guided UX for Hajj and Umrah workflow management, personal progress, private notes, and user-created travel checklists. It does not redistribute religious guidance.

## Religious-content boundary

All Hajj and Umrah step records are explicitly marked `unavailable` in NoorTools. The app does not bundle or reproduce Hajj/Umrah rulings, Ihram rules, Miqat guidance, Tawaf/Sa'i instructions, Mina/Arafat/Muzdalifah/Jamarat rulings, Talbiyah, Duas, prohibited acts, or scholarly opinions.

A current official Ministry of Hajj and Umrah Umrah guide exists and is used only as an external reference link. Nusuk's published Terms of Use state that platform text, images, graphics and other materials are owned by the Ministry or its licensors and may not be reproduced, distributed, modified, commercially used, or republished without express written permission. Therefore the guide is not bundled in this phase.

Sources:
- Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah — A Comprehensive Guide to Umrah: https://haj.gov.sa/-/media/Project/HAJJ/PDF-and-FILES/umrah-hero-guide/EN-Umrah-Guide.pdf
- Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah — Regulations and Rules: https://haj.gov.sa/en/About-the-ministry/Regulations-and-Rules
- Nusuk Umrah — Terms of Use: https://umrah.nusuk.sa/TermsOfUse
- Nusuk Hajj — Terms of Use: https://hajj.nusuk.sa/TermsOfUse

License status for all linked source material: `external_reference_only` in NoorTools. No redistribution grant was established for copying the source content into the application.

## User data

Hajj/Umrah personal progress, notes, and travel checklists are local-only user content. No checklist item is presented as a religious requirement. No progress is seeded or inferred from time, location, or navigation.

## Scholar review

No scholar-reviewed content is included. No scholar verification is claimed.

## Android

Physical Android verification is not performed in this environment and remains `NOT PHYSICALLY VERIFIED`.
